Chicken Tikka Masala

Description

Marinated chicken grilled and simmered in a rich, spiced tomato-cream sauce. Best served with basmati rice or warm naan. Plan for about 20 minutes of prep, an hour of marinating, and 30 minutes of cooking, serving 4.

Ingredients

For the chicken marinade

  • 500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Salt, to taste

For the sauce

  • 2 tbsp butter or ghee
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, for garnish

Steps

  1. Combine the chicken with all marinade ingredients in a bowl. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight for best flavor.
  2. Thread or lay the marinated chicken on a hot grill pan or skillet and cook until charred and cooked through, about 8–10 minutes. Set aside.
  3. In a large pan, melt the butter and sauté the onion until soft and golden, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger, cooking for another minute until fragrant.
  5. Stir in the garam masala and coriander, cooking for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
  6. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Stir in the cream and simmer for another 5 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
  8. Add the cooked chicken to the sauce and simmer for 5 more minutes to warm through.
  9. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with rice or naan.
