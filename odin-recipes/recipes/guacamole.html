Classic Guacamole

Description

Simple, fresh, and ready in about ten minutes with no cooking required. Great as a dip or a topping for tacos, and serves about 4.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 cup diced white onion
  • 3 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)

Steps

  1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the lime juice and salt, then mash with a fork to your desired consistency.
  3. Fold in the onion, cilantro, tomatoes, and garlic.
  4. Add a pinch of cayenne pepper if you'd like some heat.
  5. Taste and adjust salt or lime juice as needed.
  6. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, or press plastic wrap directly onto the surface and refrigerate if serving later.
