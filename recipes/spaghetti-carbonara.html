Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

A classic Roman pasta dish made with eggs, hard cheese, cured pork, and black pepper. No cream needed — the sauce comes together from the eggs and starchy pasta water. It comes together in about 25 minutes and serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 150g pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 3 large eggs
  • 50g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
  • 50g Parmesan, finely grated
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, fry the pancetta in a large skillet over medium heat until crisp, about 5–7 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, Pecorino, Parmesan, and a generous amount of black pepper.
  4. Reserve about a cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti.
  5. Add the hot spaghetti to the skillet with the pancetta, off the heat, and toss to combine.
  6. Pour in the egg and cheese mixture, tossing quickly and continuously so the eggs turn creamy instead of scrambling. Add splashes of the reserved pasta water as needed to loosen the sauce.
  7. Serve immediately with extra cheese and black pepper on top.
